Faction symbols

Symbols identify factions and traditions; their position does not date their adoption. Select a symbol to explore its stream.

Independent worlds and other powers group many societies, so they have no single faction insignia. Shared Jedi, Sith, and Imperial marks do not merge separate organizations or continuities.

What the widths mean

Widths are an editorial, qualitative interpretation of political and military reach. They are not canonical territory, population, percentages, or a quantitative dataset. The independent-worlds band groups many distinct societies for readability.

Two different kinds of influence

The narrow Jedi and Sith tracks on the left show the continuity and visibility of Force traditions, independently of the political streams. A thin line can represent survival in hiding. It does not count Force users.

Time is continuous, spacing is elastic

Dates use BBY (before the Battle of Yavin) and ABY (after it). Yavin is the zero point. The scale expands crowded years and compresses quiet centuries; equal distances do not mean equal durations. Events sharing a year are ordered within that year for readability, not dated to an invented month.

Scope

This first canon atlas covers 500 BBY through 35 ABY. It selects major events from the High Republic, films, and connected series. Navigation chapters use editorial boundaries to keep the map readable; official story eras can overlap. Legends is a separate continuity and is not mixed into this map. Open-ended traditions continue beyond the map; historical starting dates are not implied by the top edge.

Film and TV spans

Select Films & TV to reveal a companion track on the same vertical time scale. Gold diamonds mark films; blue or green brackets mark series. Each title or grouped card has a colored anchor dot fixed at its true timeline position, and every card prints its date or span. Labels may shift to avoid collisions, but the anchor never moves. Dashed lines indicate approximate dates or anthology gaps. Titles sharing a date are grouped into a single expandable record. Same-year placements do not prove scene-by-scene simultaneity.

Canon lifelines

Select Lifelines, then choose a character group. Each line follows one life, independently of political influence. A filled circle marks birth, a cross marks confirmed death, and an open circle marks the last dated appearance included here with a later fate left unknown. An upward chevron means a birth falls before the map. Dashed endpoints are approximate. Force-ghost appearances do not extend a biological lifespan; Ahsoka’s temporary death on Mortis is noted in her record.

Legends extensions

Use the continuity selector for the ancient past and distant future beyond this atlas’s core canon range. Legends has its own timeline. It is not a prequel or sequel appended to Disney canon, and a sparsely covered canon period does not make a Legends account canonical. The chart keeps these stories in separately labeled, hatched sections.
